DUBAI BEACH AND MARAFA EXCURSION

Dubai Beach & Marafa (Hell's Kitchen) Excursion

Experience one of the most exciting coastal adventures in Kenya. From the crystal-clear waters of Dubai Beach to the magical sandstone canyons of Marafa, this Mombasa tours excursion is perfect for adventure lovers, couples, families, and photographers.

Activities at Dubai Beach

  • Horse riding along the beach
  • Quad bike riding
  • Dirt bike riding
  • Sabaki River Camp Gedenge
  • Camel riding experience
  • Swimming in ocean waters
  • Beach relaxation and sunbathing
  • Photography and drone shots (add-on)
  • Beach games and fun activities
  • Perfect spots for social media content

Activities at Marafa (Hell's Kitchen)

  • Guided canyon tour
  • Sunset viewing experience
  • Learn the history and local legends of Marafa
  • Explore the stunning natural rock formations
  • Photography at the famous Hell's Kitchen viewpoint
  • Enjoy breathtaking panoramic views

What's Included

  • Comfortable transport
  • Professional guide
  • Scenic stopovers
  • Fun and memorable experience

Price: 85 Euros per person (subject to seasonality)
